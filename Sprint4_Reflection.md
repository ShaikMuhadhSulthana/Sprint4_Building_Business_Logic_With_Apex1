# Sprint 4 Reflection

## Q1. Why should software reject an application instead of allowing the Placement Officer to reject it later?

Software should automatically reject invalid applications because it reduces manual effort, prevents mistakes, and ensures business rules are followed consistently.

---

## Q2. Problems if every student can apply for every company?

Problems:

- Eligible students may lose opportunities
- Recruiters receive irrelevant applications
- Placement team workload increases
- System data becomes inaccurate

---

## Q3. Which decisions should be automatic?

## Automatic Decisions

- CGPA verification
- Backlog checking
- Deadline checking
- Duplicate application checking


## Placement Officer Decisions

- Final approval
- Company-specific exceptions
- Special cases

---

## Q4. Five Future Business Rules

1. Student cannot attend multiple interviews at the same time
